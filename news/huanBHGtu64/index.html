What Ancient Texts Say About Diabetes, From Charaka Samhita to China and Korea

Thursday, 1 October 2026 · Full Story · Hindi news by Daily Bharat News

Series: Health & Lifestyle

Watch on YouTube Subscribe

Summary

What do the ancient texts say about diabetes (madhumeha)? This episode reads what the Charaka Samhita, Sushruta Samhita, Ashtanga Hridaya, the Yoga Sutras and old Chinese and Korean medical texts write about its causes, food and daily routine, with every reference, and explains in simple words how food, activity, calm and sleep act on the body. Six yoga asanas and two pranayamas from a diabetes yoga practice are shown one by one.

Part of the Health & Lifestyle series.
